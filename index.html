About the company

enlightening.sk s.r.o. builds and operates its own digital products and online services. This site is an overview of the projects, a contact point, and a source of basic company details.

Projects

These are the projects the company operates. Each link goes to the project's own site.

  • SwingPuzzles — free online 3D puzzles

    A free puzzle game in the browser, with nothing to download. Each day adds a historical moment: you assemble the picture from pieces with real depth, then read the story, facts, and context. The game is available in several languages, and you can turn your own photo into a personalized puzzle gift.

    A daily fact and a small puzzle by email

  • Cítim, teda som — online sessions on relationships and family

    Individual online sessions with Michal Mihálik on relationships, family, children, and other life topics. A session is a place to be heard. When it fits, it can continue with gentle family-constellation work. Meetings are online and last about an hour.

  • Rodinný sebarozvoj — courses and webinars for parents

    Courses, webinars, and personal guidance with Eva Miháliková for highly sensitive people, children, and their parents. Topics include conscious parenting, boundaries, emotions, motherhood, and family relationships, from online courses to circles and rituals.
